ELDEN INTEL

EXTENSION READY

Elden Intel Interact is configured for this channel. Launch EldenIntel on the broadcaster PC to connect viewer effects to the game.

  1. Launch Elden Ring through the configured Mod Engine profile.
  2. Open EldenIntel and confirm its Twitch relay is online.
  3. Use Live Control to arm, pause, or clear viewer interactions.

Only the approved streamer can install this extension. Viewers interact directly from the channel overlay.
