ELDEN INTEL INTERACT

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Information processed

The extension receives the channel identifier, role, and opaque viewer identifier contained in Twitch Extension authorization tokens. Viewer effect requests, clone-vote ballots, cooldowns, and shared queue state are processed in memory while the broadcaster relay is running.

How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate extension requests, enforce one vote per viewer and other cooldowns, show shared interaction state, and deliver approved interactions to the broadcaster’s EldenIntel application.

Storage and sharing

Elden Intel Interact does not sell viewer information, build advertising profiles, or persist viewer effect or voting history. Temporary vote, queue, and cooldown information is discarded when the broadcaster relay stops. Twitch may process information according to Twitch’s own privacy policy.

Security

Public requests require a valid Twitch-signed authorization token. Game-effect delivery uses a separate private credential stored on the broadcaster’s Windows account.

Contact

Privacy and support contact information is provided on the extension’s Twitch listing.
