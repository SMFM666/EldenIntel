ELDEN INTEL INTERACT

TERMS OF USE

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Purpose

Elden Intel Interact lets viewers request entertainment effects and participate in shared votes during an authorized broadcaster’s Elden Ring stream. Interactions are queued, rate-limited, and may be paused or rejected by the broadcaster.

No guarantee of execution

An interaction may be unavailable because the game, EldenIntel, or the broadcaster relay is offline; because a cooldown is active; or because game state makes execution unsafe. Vote results may be resolved randomly when tied. Requests and votes have no cash value.

Viewer conduct

Viewers may not automate requests or votes, attempt to bypass cooldowns, interfere with the service, impersonate another viewer, or use the extension unlawfully.

Game and platform notice

Elden Intel Interact is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FromSoftware, Bandai Namco Entertainment, or Twitch. Use is subject to Twitch’s terms and community guidelines.

Changes and availability

Features, cooldowns, and availability may change as the extension is tested and improved. The broadcaster may disable interactions at any time.
